## Export from site

1. Open `/business-cards` in the browser.
2. Scroll to **Tommy O'Shea — Luxury landscape**.
3. Use **Card PDF** (trim 85×55mm), **Proof PDF** (A4), or **PNG** per face.

## SVG overlays (print vendor)

- `gold-curves.svg` — foil curve guides
- `celtic-tile.svg` — emboss pattern tile
- `mercedes-silhouette.svg` — spot UV / deboss van mark

## Print finishes (spec for printer)

- Spot UV on crest (front)
- Gold foil on headline + dividers
- Embossed shamrock (front corner)
- Soft-touch laminate both sides
- QR: high contrast ivory on onyx panel (back)

## CMYK note

Browser exports are RGB. Provide these hex values to your print shop for FOGRA39 / ISO Coated v2 conversion, or request a calibrated CMYK proof before press.
